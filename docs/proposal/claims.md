# Proposal claims — the author's pole (Phase 1a)

Every claim the proposal makes about what the system does, guarantees, permits, forbids, or aspires to. One row per claim. **Quote** is verbatim from the cited path; the only normalisation is that line breaks inside a multi-line `.tau` clause are collapsed to single spaces and interior indentation dropped (the line range is given so the exact layout can be checked). Pipes inside quotes are escaped as `\|`.

**Kind** ∈ {guarantee, obligation, permission, prohibition, definition-as-claim, aspiration}:
- *guarantee* — the text says something *will hold / is ensured* under stated conditions;
- *obligation* — "must", "shall", "required", "duty";
- *permission* — "may", "can", "the right to";
- *prohibition* — "may not", "prohibited", "never";
- *definition-as-claim* — a definition that asserts a property of the system rather than naming a construct;
- *aspiration* — a statement of purpose or outcome with no mechanism attached.

**Concepts** are the `###` names in `concepts.md`. Nothing here is evaluated; the brief (§2a) forbids it in this pass. Claims from the room template's amendments (the author, 2026) are in §14 and cite
