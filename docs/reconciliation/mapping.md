**Citations.** Proposal: `path:l.N`. Quotes are verbatim; line breaks inside multi-line `.tau` clauses are collapsed to single spaces.

**Verdicts (exactly one per row):**

| verdict | meaning |
|---|---|
| `direct` | a Tau construct expresses it as written; only spelling changes |
| `expressible with work` | expressible in v0.7 Tau, but needs a design decision, a reinterpretation, an ADT, a host-side piece (tau-testnet style), or composition |
| `needs a faculty` | expressible only once a tool/library/model/discipline we do not have exists (see `faculties.md`, F-ids) |
| `not expressible` | not expressible in Tau as it stands — the Notes say why: decidability, the time-compatibility quantification pattern, the constant/stream distinction, no referent in any base algebra, or the object lives outside the language (persons, intent, licences) |
| `TML-only` | the proposal names a TML construct with no Tau counterpart (cite `HISTORY.md §3`) |

**Labels:** FACT = the cited text says it; INFERENCE = my reading; SPECULATION = a conjecture the oracle must settle. The label applies to the *verdict*; the existence of each cited construct is FACT.

**Tau constructs used** (all FACT, tau-lang README): `always`/`[]` and `sometimes`/`<>` over `local_spec` (l.321-347); input/output streams `i…[t]`, `o…[t]`, lookback `[t-k]`, fixed `[k]` (l.1313-1397); the `tau` type and `{ spec } : tau` constants — "a Tau specification that itself has a Tau specification as a constant" (l.1235-1265); `sbf`, `bv[n]` (l.1441-1470); pointwise revision through `u` (l.1722-1856); the `this` input stream (l.1806-1812); recurrence relations and `normalize … fallback` (l.1137-1233); ADTs `type X = {…}`, inheritance, tuple-typed streams (l.1569-1690); constant time constraints `[t <= 3]` (l.1691-1720); uninterpreted constants `<name:type>` (l.1427-1437); satisfiability as time-compatible quantification (l.460-487); REPL `sat`/`unsat`/`valid`/`solve`/`normalize` (l.2215-2241); the Python binding exposes `get_interpreter`/`step`/`current_spec` only (`bindings/python/nanobind/tau_nanobind.cpp:l.147-180`) — `sat` is **not** exposed (confirmed independently by `tau-testnet/README.md:l.553`).

## Report to the author

**Bottom line.** The proposal is expressible in Tau v0.7 in its *mechanics* and not in its *persons*. Self-amendment, recursion, contradiction detection, the time dimension and "the law of changing the law" map directly or nearly so (M015, M024, M034, M046, M052, M069). Everything that locates meaning, weight, agency, dissent or authorship in a person — Being, weight, natural seeds, the alignment cycle, non-retroactive silencing — has no Tau counterpart and needs the layer Los Tres Soles already describes.

**Counts (134 rows).** direct 20 · expressible with work 54 · needs a faculty 28 · not expressible 30 · TML-only 2 · pending whitepaper 4.

**Three gaps that most change the plan.**
1. **G09 — revision is not reconciliation.** The newest satisfiable update wins and nothing dropped is recorded. Reconciling (judgment, weight, minority report) must run *above* `u`; the spec-diff (F3) is the minority report's logical content. Revision is the apply step.
2. **G04/M010 — opinion maps carry direction, not weight.** On all evidence in corpus, agreement is a logical relation; the only magnitude that runs is one validator, one vote. Weight lives in our layer (F7). Pending whitepaper.
3. **G05 — a seed is a key-holder.** The river seats as a record now; it acts only when someone holds its key.
